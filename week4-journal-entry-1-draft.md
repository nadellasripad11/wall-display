# wall display: ideating and drafting the bom

spent 10 hours figuring out the wall display and putting the bom together.

i started double sided, then switched to single sided because i wanted a bigger screen. it is basically a bigger iphone for your wall. it shows apps, and you can pick split screen or one app full screen right on the display.

i liked the round displays more, but the bigger ones were too expensive, so i went with the 7 inch waveshare esp32-s3 board with touch for $38.99. it plugs into the wall and just stays on.

rest of the bom is a charger, cable, switch cable, wall mount, and sticky strips. dropped the power bank because it would blow the budget, and no frame because the mount already hangs it.

bom is $92.59 at sale prices before shipping and tax. still gotta confirm the wall, the outlet distance, and the real checkout totals before ordering anything. next up is the repo, schematic, and firmware.
